## Proposed deterministic rule

This is a mechanical contract to challenge before implementation; it contains no approved numerical profile. The first independent challenge found historical lookahead and event-restart ambiguity; the revised rules below address those findings.

1. Inputs are GBP/JPY chronological lower-timeframe setup bars, completed higher-timeframe context bars, a sequenced bid/ask quote evidence window, caller-versioned session/regime context, provider/environment, and source/build identity. Bars must be complete, source-identified, valid, ordered, on-grid and before their evaluation cutoff. V1 accepts provider-native bars only; derived aggregation fails closed as `derived_lineage_unverified` until constituent lineage can be verified. Dataset/snapshot identity is input evidence, not part of the immutable strategy-profile digest.
2. A swing high/low is a strict unique extremum over configured left/right completed bars. Lower- and higher-timeframe swing profiles have separate required left/right confirmation counts. Equal extrema are never pivots in V1. A pivot becomes knowable only after all right-side confirmation bars close; record pivot time and confirmation time.
3. At each candidate break close, recompute higher-timeframe alignment using only context bars and pivots confirmed by that close. Bullish alignment requires the latest four pivots to alternate low-high-low-high, with both the second low and second high higher than their respective predecessors. Bearish alignment requires high-low-high-low, with both the second high and second low lower. A bar qualifying as both a strict high and strict low is ignored as a pivot; duplicate pivot confirmation timestamps invalidate context. Missing, mixed, stale, equal or non-alternating context is ineligible. Session/regime measurement timestamps must also be no later than the break close. Record the context cutoff and source IDs.
4. A break event is the first completed lower-timeframe close beyond the latest eligible lower-timeframe swing high (long) or low (short), confirmed before the break, in the aligned direction, by the configured minimum displacement measured against ATR from bars completed before the break. Freeze swing ID/level, HTF context and pre-break ATR at that close. Later closes beyond the same swing cannot restart/extend the event; another event needs another confirmed swing. The break bar is not a pullback or confirmation bar.
5. Freeze the pullback zone at the break. For either direction, it is the interval around the broken level with half-width `pullbackToleranceATR × frozenBreakATR`. For every post-break bar, check completed-close invalidation first; if invalidation and zone touch occur on the same bar, invalidation wins. Otherwise the first completed bar whose wick intersects the zone is the only pullback considered. Its close must remain beyond the configured invalidation boundary (broken level minus/plus `invalidationBufferATR × frozenBreakATR`). A later touch cannot replace the first pullback; wick intersection and boundary inclusivity are fixed/versioned. `maximumBarsFromBreak` counts completed lower-timeframe bars strictly after the break; the first-touch bar is included, and a first touch on the limit bar is allowed.
6. The stop's structural extreme is calculated over the inclusive interval from the first-touch bar through the bar immediately before confirmation (the confirmation bar is excluded): minimum low for long, maximum high for short. After pullback, the first completed lower-timeframe bar closing above `max(brokenLevel, precedingCompletedHigh) + continuationBufferATR × frozenBreakATR` (long) or below `min(brokenLevel, precedingCompletedLow) - continuationBufferATR × frozenBreakATR` (short) confirms. `maximumBarsAfterPullback` counts completed bars strictly after the pullback; a trigger on the final allowed bar is eligible. This is the only confirmation-window limit. If no trigger arrives within that window, expire. The confirmation bar cannot close through structural invalidation; no intra-bar order is inferred.
7. Freeze stop beyond the defined pullback extreme by the configured buffer using frozen pre-break ATR. If the confirmation bar already crosses that stop, return `STOP_TOUCHED_BEFORE_ENTRY`. The one V1 target method is a required fixed R multiple; invalid direction/order is rejected before quote evaluation.
8. Quote evidence must provide the provider's ordered observations from confirmation-bar end through entry, with instrument/provider/environment, monotonic source sequence, source/receipt timestamps, bid/ask and a completeness attestation under the configured sampling policy. Unknown gaps return `ENTRY_INTERVAL_UNRESOLVED`; sampled data cannot claim completeness unless its source attests it. The selected quote is the first observation with source time strictly later than confirmation-bar end; receipt is at/after source and no later than evaluation. Both source age (`evaluationTime-sourceTime`) and receipt age (`evaluationTime-receivedAt`) meet configured bounds. Reject if another completed setup bar falls between confirmation and selected quote. Use ask long/bid short. Adverse drift is from confirmation close: `max(0, ask-confirmationClose)` long or `max(0, confirmationClose-bid)` short. Reject if any quote in the complete window crossed the structural stop, or selected executable side is through it. The plan is not a fill.
9. Price inputs must align exactly to required `priceTickSize`; otherwise reject. Preserve entry quote exactly. Round the structural stop outward from entry to the tick grid (floor for long stop, ceiling for short stop). Derive fixed-R target from entry and the rounded stop distance, then round target conservatively toward entry (floor for long target, ceiling for short target). Validate level ordering after rounding. Compute reward-to-cost after rounding as `gross target reward pips / (observed spread pips + configured additional round-trip cost pips)`, where gross reward is `(target-entryAsk)/pipSize` for long and `(entryBid-target)/pipSize` for short. Denominator must be finite and positive; reject below the configured minimum. Preserve bid/ask, source/as-of/receipt timestamps, raw/rounded levels and cost terms.
10. Session and regime eligibility is checked at break, first touch, confirmation and selected quote. Every point must fall inside an explicitly allowed session and carry an allowed regime label. Session close expires an active event; it cannot carry over. Explicit `.allSessions` or `.allRegimes` is allowed, but the versioned session calendar and regime context are still required. Measurement timestamps must precede each event point.
11. Caller-versioned session/regime context carries digest, calendar/session identity, timezone/DST interpretation, measurement time, regime formula/version, allowed labels and source IDs. Missing/mismatched context fails closed. Provider-native bars require verifiable source IDs; caller labels alone do not prove derived lineage. Output preserves pinned engine/build/code identity, dataset/snapshot digests, canonical config/input digests, and every source ID used.
12. Canonical immutable profile digest is SHA-256 over canonical sorted-key encoding of strategy version and rule/profile fields only; it excludes per-evaluation source snapshots and quotes. Canonical input digest is SHA-256 over profile digest; instrument/provider/environment/timeframes; exact fields and IDs of source bars used; quote sequence/values/IDs/timestamps; and session/regime context digests/source IDs. Stable event ID uses strategy version, immutable profile digest, instrument/timeframe, frozen swing ID, first break-bar ID and direction. New quote snapshots change input/decision digest but cannot create a second event for the same break.
13. Evaluator emits only this event's deterministic ID and interval `[breakBarEnd, terminalEvaluationTime]`; it does not claim overlap grouping or emit a cluster key. The research campaign computes clusters from intervals using its separately preregistered grouping policy, prior event set, and cooldown. The evaluator does not enforce cross-event cooldown or cluster exclusion.
14. Maximum bars from break, maximum bars after pullback, separate lower/HTF swing counts, `continuationBufferATR`, ATR lookback, timeframe pair, grid origin, provider/environment, pip size/price tick size, quote completeness/source/receipt age, spread, drift, estimated additional round-trip cost, reward-to-cost, allowed session/regime policies and every buffer/threshold are explicit required config. V1 ATR is the simple mean over the configured completed lookback of `max(high-low, abs(high-previousClose), abs(low-previousClose))`. Missing intervals inside an active session reject; expected closure gaps are determined only by the required versioned calendar, and no event crosses session close. No silent defaults or Live profile.

## Event selection correction — 2026-11-13

Independent implementation review found that the original evaluator API accepted a full candle snapshot but returned one decision without specifying which break event to evaluate. Choosing the globally earliest or latest event would introduce an unregistered selection rule and could let an old expired event hide later setups. The accepted per-event contract is therefore:

1. Discovery takes an explicit inclusive lower-bar window identified by its first and last source bar IDs. Both IDs must occur exactly once and define an ordered slice; bars outside that slice may provide prior causal swing/ATR context, but cannot be break candidates. Discovery also takes an explicit immutable prior-event cursor containing consumed `(frozenSwingID, direction)` keys and its digest; an empty cursor must be explicit. It returns full break descriptors in chronological order: frozen swing ID, direction, break-bar ID, strategy/profile identity, exact window endpoints/digest, cursor digest, and stable event ID.
2. Each event is the first qualifying completed close per unconsumed frozen swing and direction, where the frozen swing must still be the latest eligible confirmed swing of that type/direction at that break close. Older swings superseded by a newer confirmed swing are skipped/retired even if their keys are not consumed. Repeated closes past the same swing do not create another event. A swing high can produce a long event and a swing low can produce a short event. The campaign persists the consumed swing/direction key on first event discovery and supplies the updated cursor to later windows; moving a window forward without that cursor is invalid campaign use and cannot authorize re-emission.
3. Evaluation requires the full caller-selected break descriptor and the exact same inclusive evaluation window and cursor snapshot used for discovery. It verifies the descriptor's window digest/endpoints and cursor digest, then verifies the descriptor is a qualifying first break for that swing/direction in that window and is not already consumed before evaluating only that event. Unknown, ambiguous, out-of-window, mismatched, or non-first descriptors fail closed. Pending, expired, rejected, and signal results retain the same event identity and causal source provenance. The stable event ID is bound to event/profile identity; cursor and window digests remain separate input provenance. The input digest binds only the exact causal bars, contexts and quote observations consumed for that event plus window/cursor identity, never unrelated future observations.
4. Independent events may overlap. Candidate enumeration does not rank or suppress distinct swing events; campaign/selector owns overlap clustering and ranking under a separately registered policy. A later distinct swing event remains evaluable even when an earlier event has expired or invalidated. Re-arm on the same swing/direction is forbidden; a distinct newly confirmed swing has a distinct key. Campaign cursor advancement is atomic and idempotent: it records event ID plus consumed key before advancing the evaluation window; exact event retries deduplicate by event ID, and persistence must reject stale cursor-version advancement. The evaluator validates only the supplied cursor's canonical digest and descriptor binding; it cannot know whether that cursor is current without an authoritative store. Until `STRAT-GBPJPY-TREND-CAMPAIGN-CURSOR` passes, do not run a campaign or claim stale-cursor rejection across concurrent/relaunched callers.

Acceptance fixtures must cover two distinct swings with overlapping windows, repeated closes over one swing, an unconsumed older swing superseded by a newer confirmed swing, a later window with the prior consumed-swing cursor, a newly confirmed swing after an older event expires, long/short event semantics, exclusion outside the requested window, missing/ambiguous endpoints, candidate descriptor mismatch, and evaluation of a later event after an older event expires. The campaign persistence contract must demonstrate atomic cursor advancement and idempotent duplicate event insertion before any campaign is run. This correction makes event enumeration and re-arm explicit; it supplies no production profile values and does not authorize Live use.

## Campaign cursor persistence design proposal — 2026-11-13

This proposal is awaiting an independent design challenge before implementation. It uses the existing local research archive and adds no dependency.

1. Add `GBPJPYTrendPullbackCampaignStore` as a separate local research component. Each campaign is scoped by strategy/profile/config digest, engine/build identity, instrument, provider/environment, timeframe pair, and versioned session identity. It is not called by Live selectors, notifications, orders, or the Mac Trade screen.
2. Persist one versioned campaign snapshot containing its immutable definition, current revision, current canonical discovery cursor, active-event set, next expected discovery-window start, and the complete ordered transaction journal. A transaction contains the expected prior revision/cursor digest, inclusive new-discovery window and its digest, all newly discovered descriptors (including overlapping distinct swings), an evaluation for every active event plus each new descriptor, the updated active-event set, updated cursor, next expected discovery boundary, and transaction digest. Empty discovery windows are persisted as checkpoints and still evaluate active events.
3. Every initialize/read/commit operation acquires an OS advisory exclusive lock on a campaign-specific lock file, then rereads and validates the complete snapshot while holding the lock. Commit checks exact transaction retry first; identical retry returns the original receipt, while reuse of the same transaction identity with different content returns a conflict. It then compares expected revision and cursor digest with current state, rejects stale revisions, validates window progression and profile scope, and writes the complete new snapshot using `AtomicFile.write` before releasing the lock. The lock is required because `ResearchStore` actor isolation is per instance and its existing writes do not provide cross-instance/process CAS.
4. Separate the immutable discovery provenance from each evaluation horizon. Every active event retains its original descriptor, discovery window/cursor digests, and immutable discovery input evidence. Each later transaction revalidates that descriptor against the original discovery evidence, then deterministically reevaluates the event using all completed bars and quotes through the new as-of time. Stable event ID and original discovery provenance remain fixed; the evaluation input digest changes as the horizon advances. WAIT events remain active; terminal events leave the active set only under the explicit classification table below.
5. Window progression is strictly chronological within one versioned session campaign. The next discovery window must begin at the persisted expected bar boundary; gaps require an explicitly recorded session-close boundary and terminate that campaign. A new session uses a new campaign identity. The store cannot infer skipped market data from opaque IDs, so each transaction carries ordered complete candidate-window bar IDs/timestamps and validates exact timeframe adjacency. Active-event evaluation may reuse prior source bars and add later bars without changing the original discovery window/cursor.
6. Discovery consumes every newly discovered `(frozenSwingID, direction)` key in a committed window regardless of the resulting decision state; exact event retries deduplicate, but a different overlapping swing remains active and in the journal. The pure evaluator continues validating only the caller-supplied immutable discovery cursor snapshot; store commit owns freshness and atomic advancement.
7. Corrupt, missing, malformed, or revision-inconsistent snapshots fail closed and are never silently reset. The single-snapshot replacement ensures cursor and event journal advance together; retaining a full journal per campaign is acceptable for this bounded initial research store, with compaction excluded until measured growth requires it.

Before campaign-store implementation, independent acceptance must pass `STRAT-GBPJPY-TREND-ACTIVE-CONTINUATION`: one WAIT event advances to a signal or expiry on later bars; original discovery descriptor and cursor remain stable; quote refresh changes input digest but not event ID; re-evaluation after restart reproduces the same result; two overlapping active events progress independently; and missing/stale/malformed observations do not terminally discard active events.

Store acceptance must cover two independent store instances racing on one base revision (exactly one commit wins), stale revision rejection, exact retry after constructing a new store instance, same transaction ID with changed content, empty-window checkpoint/relaunch while active events still advance, overlapping descriptors, malformed/out-of-order windows, wrong campaign/profile identity, corrupt snapshot behavior, and failure injection around atomic snapshot replacement. The transaction ID is canonical over campaign ID, expected revision, prior cursor digest, discovery-window digest, and all evaluation input digests. Snapshot validation replays a contiguous revision/journal/cursor/window chain. A single snapshot is acceptable only with its process-crash guarantee documented; no power-loss durability claim unless file and directory flushes are implemented. Lock contention must be bounded or reported and must release on descriptor close. Until both continuation and store acceptance plus independent review pass, the cursor row remains BACKLOG and campaigns must not run.

### Event continuation reason classification

Before store implementation, replace string-only discovery/evaluation outcomes with typed contracts:

- Discovery returns exactly one of `completeWithEvents(descriptors)`, `completeEmpty`, `retryableDataQuality(reason, inputDigest)`, or `integrityRejected(reason)`. `completeEmpty` is valid only after all bars/context needed for discovery, identities, cursor/window, session grid, and provenance pass validation. Only the two `complete*` cases may advance the discovery boundary. A `retryableDataQuality` attempt may be journaled with the same expected next window and unchanged cursor, but must not consume the window or event keys. Integrity rejection must not mutate campaign state.
- The discovery request explicitly carries the complete lower-timeframe candidate window, causal pre-window bars needed for swings/ATR, completed higher-timeframe context bars, versioned session/regime context, `asOf`, exact configuration, inclusive window IDs, prior cursor, and source provenance (provider, environment, build/code identity, snapshot digest, provider-native lineage). Quote observations and quote-window completeness are not discovery inputs; those belong to event evaluation. The typed batch preserves instrument/provider/environment/profile/window/cursor digests, `asOf`, source IDs, and diagnostic reason codes. A measured disallowed session/regime is a valid complete result with zero eligible descriptors and diagnostics. Missing context or incomplete source lineage is not a complete-empty result.
- Event evaluation returns a typed reason code and disposition, preserving the stable `eventID`, original discovery window/cursor digest, and current evaluation input digest on every active-event result, including early data-quality holds. Wrong candidate/event/profile/cursor/window identity is a transaction error; it cannot be converted into an event result.
- Explicitly distinguish `CONTEXT_DATA_UNAVAILABLE` from `CONTEXT_MEASURED_DISALLOWED`, and `ENTRY_INTERVAL_UNRESOLVED` from `ENTRY_TOO_LATE`. Missing/late-arriving source evidence may be retried; a measured disallowed context or missed first-quote opportunity has a stable, terminal outcome under the frozen profile.

The store never infers disposition from free-form text. The V1 mapping preserves first-quote and no-late-entry rules:

|---|---|---|
| `AWAITING_FIRST_PULLBACK`, `AWAITING_CONTINUATION` | ACTIVE | Reevaluate on later completed bars; bar-count expiry uses validated completed bars only. |
| Missing/stale quote, incomplete quote window, unavailable/missing/stale/malformed bar or context, temporarily unavailable provenance that can be reacquired without changing campaign identity | ACTIVE with data-quality status | Record diagnostic/input digest, but do not consume a bar deadline or expire from local wall time. Retry when a complete valid source snapshot arrives. |
| `candidate_descriptor_mismatch`, `candidate_break_unknown`, `candidate_break_out_of_window`, `candidate_break_not_first_eligible_event`, cursor/window mismatch, wrong provider/environment/profile/build identity, bar/quote identity mismatch | Transaction rejected / campaign integrity error | Do not change cursor, active set, or revision; these are not market outcomes. |
| `derived_lineage_unverified` | Discovery transaction rejected / campaign integrity error | This profile requires provider-native lineage. Do not advance this campaign with derived bars. |
| Duplicate/non-monotonic quote sequence or source-time order | Discovery/evaluation integrity rejection | The window cannot be deterministically replayed. Do not advance cursor or event state. |
| `bars_invalid_or_unordered`, `missing_grid_interval`, malformed but identity-consistent quote/bar evidence, `provenance_missing`, missing/stale/malformed context or quote | ACTIVE with retryable data-quality status | Preserve event identity and diagnostic; do not count invalid bars, consume bar deadlines, or advance discovery window/cursor. Reevaluate after a complete valid snapshot. |
| `CONTEXT_MEASURED_DISALLOWED` | TERMINAL eligibility rejection | A fresh, version-matched context explicitly measures an unallowed session or regime at an event checkpoint. |

Preserve the rejection and do not signal. |
| `STRUCTURAL_INVALIDATION`, `FIRST_TOUCH_INVALIDATED` | TERMINAL setup rejection | Frozen structural conditions have failed. |
| `PULLBACK_WINDOW_EXPIRED`, `CONFIRMATION_WINDOW_EXPIRED` | TERMINAL expiry | Expiry is determined from validated completed bars in the same session, not missing data or wall-clock delay alone. |
| `SESSION_CLOSED` | TERMINAL expiry | Versioned calendar confirms session close; no event carries across sessions. |
| `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` / `.signal` | TERMINAL plan result | Store the immutable research plan and event; it is not a fill or order. |
| `CONFIGURATION_INCOMPLETE`, `CONFIGURATION_INVALID`, `provenance_invalid`, `cursor_invalid`, `evaluation_window_invalid`, wrong instrument/provider/environment or profile digest | Campaign initialization or transaction rejected | No campaign window/cursor change. Missing config cannot make an empty result; a profile or identity mismatch cannot be retried under the same campaign identity. |
| `ENTRY_INTERVAL_UNRESOLVED` caused by an incomplete/missing quote sample | ACTIVE with retryable data-quality status | No late-entry conclusion until the complete source window is attested. |
| `ENTRY_TOO_LATE` or completed intervening bar before the first eligible quote | TERMINAL risk/plan rejection | The frozen first-quote and no-late-entry rule has been missed. |
| `STOP_DIRECTION_INVALID`, `ROUNDED_LEVEL_ORDER_INVALID`, `COST_DENOMINATOR_INVALID` | TERMINAL plan rejection | Frozen plan arithmetic is invalid for this event; do not keep retrying the same event/profile. |
| Stop touched before entry | TERMINAL plan rejection | The structural stop was already crossed before the plan could be formed. |
| `PRICE_OFF_TICK` on a selected observation in a complete quote window | Evaluation held for source correction; never skip to a later quote | Retry only if the source corrects/reissues the same first observation and supplies a fresh complete snapshot; do not advance discovery or quote selection. |
