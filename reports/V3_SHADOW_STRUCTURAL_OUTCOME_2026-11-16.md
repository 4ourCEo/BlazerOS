## Debate findings

- `V3StructuralOutcomeEvaluator` already evaluates plan stop/target triggers using executable bid/ask sides. Its default quality is `.simulated`; it can also return untriggered, ambiguous, and unresolved states.
- `ResearchStore.record(_ outcome:)` validates only the standalone outcome before immutable write. It does not bind the record to the exact persisted plan and entry evidence, serialize a per-plan terminal decision across store instances, or constrain quality.
- The current Live monitor has authenticated selected-pair OANDA FX quote responses with source and receipt times. Quote samples are sparse; they cannot prove that a stop or target was untouched between polls. An untriggered result therefore cannot be treated as final without a separately designed coverage horizon.
- `V3PositionRecord.brokerClosed` requires broker identity and exit price; it has no exit time or exit observation. A market quote crossing a plan level is not a user or broker position close. The user-reported path records an outside-the-app opening statement only.
- `.observed` is valid quote-entry evidence, but an outcome with that quality can affect verified-performance aggregation elsewhere. Market-path structural outcomes must be pinned to `.simulated`.
- Independent source audits recommend a dedicated shadow-outcome writer, not the generic `record` path, with exact-plan/entry binding, root serialization, append-time stale-generation/session checks, and no position side effects.

## Proposed bounded scope

1. Persist only one simulated structural outcome for an exact validated Live OANDA plan and its exact persisted `.observed` entry record. Verify plan ID, instrument, strategy/engine versions, entry record identity/payload, entry freshness at its own receipt (maximum age 5 seconds), source time after plan creation, executable entry within the preregistered inclusive deviation band and strictly inside stop/target levels, `.oandaLive` provider/environment, and a fresh post-entry exit observation under one per-root transaction. Reject generic `record(V3PlanEntryEvidence)` in `live-oanda`; only the specialized Live entry writer may create that archive evidence.
2. Use the current selected-pair monitor quote only after it is successfully fetched and validated. Preserve OANDA source time and response receipt time. Validate entry freshness at `entry.receivedAt` and exit freshness at `exit.receivedAt`, each with a maximum age of 5 seconds. Require both `exit.sourceTime > entry.sourceTime` and `exit.receivedAt > entry.receivedAt`; do not compare a source timestamp from one observation to the receipt timestamp of another, since those are different clock domains. Per-observation freshness compares that quote's source timestamp to its own captured receipt timestamp. Recheck current generation, exact selected pair, FX mode, no scan ownership, and Live session immediately before the append.
3. Pin outcome quality to `.simulated`; use executable bid for long exits and ask for short exits. Treat a stop/target crossing as the **first sampled trigger in the available quote evidence**, not proof of the first market touch, user close, broker fill, or actual trade result. Keep the outcome copy explicitly shadow/simulated, and show “first sampled stop/target trigger” in Review. `V3StructuralOutcome` is currently read by Review and ForwardTest only; no verified-performance aggregate consumes it. This implementation must not add such an aggregate.
4. Persist only a terminal stop-loss/take-profit result from a qualifying trigger. Do not persist `.untriggered` as final because the monitor samples sparsely and has no fixed coverage horizon. If setup lifecycle becomes invalidated or expired after the entry reference, accept a stop/target trigger only when its provider source time is strictly earlier than the terminal lifecycle source time. At or after the terminal source time, or if terminal source time is missing, leave the plan unresolved and persist no structural terminal outcome. Missing, stale, mismatched, out-of-order, or ambiguous evidence also remains unavailable/unresolved without modifying plan, entry, setup lifecycle, or position records.
5. Under the per-root lock, reject invalid/corrupt outcome archives, enforce a single terminal outcome per plan, make exact retries idempotent, and reject a conflicting second terminal result. Derive a safe deterministic ID from plan identity and result identity. Failed writes leave prior evidence unchanged.
6. Keep actual position-close reporting out of this slice. A future user-reported close and any broker-sourced close require distinct provenance, timestamp, exit observation/identity, correction, and transition contracts.

## Challenge questions

- Is “first sampled trigger” safe and clear enough for `.simulated` winner/loser outcomes when OANDA quotes are polled sparsely? What stronger evidence or UI wording is required?
- The proposal now cuts off outcomes at setup invalidation/expiry: only a trigger strictly before terminal lifecycle source time can persist. Is this chronology rule deterministic and conservative enough?
- Are source-time and receipt-time strict chronology checks both implementable without mixing provider/local clock domains?
- Is terminal one-outcome-per-plan appropriate, including exact retry and conflicting trigger behavior across two store instances?
- Does the design adequately prevent quote evidence from becoming an actual position close or verified-performance record? Existing source search found no aggregate consumer of `V3StructuralOutcome`; the implementation must preserve that boundary.

## Integration evidence — 2026-11-16

- The active selected-pair Live monitor now evaluates the next fresh OANDA quote against the exact persisted validated plan and its sole persisted `.observed` entry evidence. Entry and exit observations are each validated at their own receipt with a five-second maximum. The entry is revalidated against plan chronology, provider/environment, spread, inclusive deviation band, and strict stop/target interior.
- A stop/target crossing is persisted only as a `.simulated` “first sampled stop/target trigger” result. Untriggered polls do not create a terminal outcome. The append transaction enforces one terminal outcome per plan, exact retry, conflicting-result rejection, and a generation/pair/FX/scan/Live gate. Lifecycle invalidation/expiry cuts off any trigger at or after its source time.
- Generic `record(V3PlanEntryEvidence)` and `record(V3StructuralOutcome)` reject the `live-oanda` archive, so callers cannot bypass the specialized exact-plan transactions. The generic structural evaluator retains its previous 10-second/evaluation-time behavior; only the Live shadow writer opts into entry-at-receipt freshness. No position close, broker fill, order, user-reported close, setup lifecycle event, or verified-performance aggregation is created.
