# Mac strategy signals: direction and design

Date: 2026-11-16 (America/Los_Angeles)
Status: Mac research-signal implementation integrated and staged. Focused tests, Mac Release build, signature/manifest verification, and updated app runtime scan pass. The runtime scan completed with no eligible directional candidates; Trade correctly remained WAIT.

## User problem

The Mac Trade panel says `WAIT / NO TRADE` even when research evaluators may produce a fresh directional candidate. The user wants to see strategy signals. The app currently combines two different questions: “Did a research evaluator emit a candidate?” and “Is this profile approved to publish a production Live decision?” Those answers must be made visible separately.

## Debate findings

- **Engine audit:** `LightningDeskModel.selectLiveStrategySelection` (`LightningDeskModel.swift:2465-2483`) evaluates the four Legacy control policies, Candidate001, Candidate002, and ORB. The registry has no production promotion records (`StrategyResearchRegistry.swift:417-418`). `LiveStrategySelector` requires an exact valid promotion record for a `.liveSignal` profile; when none qualify it returns `WAIT` with `no_strategy_passed_live_promotion_gate` (`LiveStrategyDecision.swift:170-205`). Tests assert this behavior. WAIT is therefore expected from the production decision layer; it does not prove that no candidate fired.
- **Research audit:** evaluator implementations also exist for Breakout Acceptance, Trend Pullback, Range Rejection, and London Break + Retest, but they are not wired into the Mac scan. Their exact production profiles and/or cost-adjusted evidence are incomplete. The strategy plans prohibit invented thresholds and automatic promotion.
- **Risk challenge:** changing a shadow candidate into a Live BUY/SELL, loosening the promotion gate, or promoting a strategy from synthetic tests would conceal an evidence gap and could make research output look executable. Candidate direction must remain separate from the Trade decision, plan, notification, CTA, and broker order path.
- **Counterproposal rejected:** promote ORB or another existing candidate solely to make BUY/SELL appear. There is no completed held-out campaign, valid production promotion record, and signed approval for that change.
- **Counterproposal rejected:** count Legacy policy variants as independent strategy confirmations. They are correlated control variants and cannot create a strategy quorum.

## Resolved plan

**Goal:** make fresh strategy-derived candidate signals visible in the Mac app while retaining the current production promotion gate and order safeguards.

**Ranked scope:**

1. Add a distinct Mac `Research Signals` surface populated from candidate results already produced during the current Live scan.
2. Show a signal only for a fresh, eligible, directional candidate with exact registered strategy identity and valid source/config/evaluator/adapter provenance. Display strategy name/version, instrument, LONG/SHORT direction, observation time/freshness, and concise source evidence/reasons.
3. Keep the official Trade status at `WAIT / NO TRADE` unless the existing promotion requirements pass. Research signals do not select a winner, combine strategies, populate the Trade plan, create signal notifications/CTAs, or authorize an order.
4. Keep additional evaluators disconnected until each has an exact frozen input/profile contract. Require campaign evidence and explicit approval before considering production promotion.

**Exclusions:** no changes to strategy thresholds, profiles, `decisionRole`, production promotion records, selector eligibility, risk sizing, order authority, OANDA credentials, or broker behavior. No iPhone UI changes. No claim of profitability or of “no setup” when an adapter emits no candidate.

## Design contract

- The Trade decision and research signal list are separate typed values. The existing `LiveStrategyDecision` and promotion gate remain authoritative for the Trade panel.
- The signal path is separately typed and ephemeral. A candidate must match the exact registered identity and `.shadow` role; provider must normalize to `OANDA-LIVE`; candidate effective-configuration SHA-256 must equal the exact per-instrument adapter configuration actually evaluated; evaluator, adapter, and selector digests must match the compiled trusted registry values. The signed build-manifest/source attestation must also be verified. `StrategyPromotionRuntime.load` currently returns the same `.empty` profiles/promotions on validation failure, so the implementation must add an explicit verified/failed attestation result and withhold all signals if verification failed; compiled profile constants alone are insufficient.
- Each signal binds to the exact current scan generation and `ScanInputSnapshot.identity` for its source candles, quote/spread, expiry, engine, and instrument. Late candidate completion from an older generation or a changed source identity cannot publish. The projector independently checks accepted spread and recomputes the 8-second quote-age bound (inclusive, with the one-second future allowance).
- Only fresh (`maximumAge` policy), eligible, non-`.wait` candidates from exact registered identities are displayed. Add optional, backward-decodable `validUntil` provenance to the candidate contract, populated by adapters from existing rules (no new thresholds): ORB uses the earlier of quote time + its existing 5s quote-freshness limit and its frozen setup expiry; Candidate001/002 use the earlier of quote time + `EntryQuality.maxAgeSeconds` (8s) and event OHLC-availability time + the already-digested configured signal TTL (300s / 120s respectively). Projection requires `now <= validUntil`; the deadline is inclusive, with the existing one-second future timestamp allowance, and becomes stale at `validUntil + 1ms`. Missing, invalid, or untrusted expiry is withheld. Tests pin exact deadline and +1ms behavior.
- A visible signal expires at that candidate-specific `validUntil`; it clears on a new scan, failed/stale refresh, instrument or mode change, app backgrounding, or expiry. Use a monotonic wait, then recheck wall-clock validity; foreground recovery never revives an expired or persisted signal.
- The connected directional evaluator coverage is only ORB, Candidate001, and Candidate002. The four Legacy variants are control inputs, not independent signal families. The UI must report this coverage honestly; it must not imply all registered strategies ran.
- A staged Mac scan remained at `EUR/USD 1/6` for several minutes while the quote loop continued. Finite OANDA HTTP calls now use a dedicated session with a 20s inactivity timeout and 30s total resource timeout; the long-lived streaming call remains on `URLSession.shared`. This bounds each HTTP leg, not the total six-market scan duration. `OandaClient` is shared, so the finite-request cap also applies to iPhone OANDA data calls; no iPhone UI or order behavior changed.
- Adapter/selector source digests are now declared in `StrategyImplementationDigests.swift`, outside the source files they identify. The adapter digest was refreshed to the actual `AdaptiveOpportunityAdapters.swift` SHA-256 so current candidates carry the current adapter provenance after the deadline/outcome changes.
- Adapter outcomes distinguish `completedNoCandidate`, `completedWithCandidates`, and `failed(reason)`. Existing `try? ... ?? []` and catch-and-continue paths collapse failures into empty arrays; those boundaries must become typed outcomes before the UI claims that no candidate was returned. A completed empty evaluation is displayed as `No eligible candidate returned by connected evaluators`, not `No setup`.
- Display every eligible candidate independently. Opposing candidates remain separate; do not rank, vote, arbitrate, or call one the winner. Use neutral `LONG CANDIDATE` / `SHORT CANDIDATE` copy with visually persistent `RESEARCH ONLY · NOT PROMOTED · NO ORDER` labels, visibly distinct from BUY/SELL.
- Do not show candidate entry/stop/target levels in this first slice. Those could be mistaken for a validated execution plan; the existing selector requires promotion and structural validation before projecting a Trade plan.
- If no eligible directional candidate is emitted, show typed evaluator coverage/results. Do not imply every strategy was evaluated; `completedNoCandidate` and `failed` must have distinct states. Neither means “no setup.”
- No research signal may schedule, restore, or emit a production signal notification. Restored historical candidates are never current signals; only a fresh scan can repopulate the surface.
- Keep implementation Mac-owned. If the shared model/selection types cannot carry this boundary without changing iPhone behavior, add a Mac-specific projection seam and test the platform guard instead of changing shared strategy semantics.

## Measurable acceptance

- A fresh exact-profile candidate projects a visible research signal with direction and provenance; `LiveStrategyDecision` remains WAIT with no plan under the empty production promotion registry.
- Spoofed/missing/mismatched configuration or code digests, invalid signed manifest, old generation, or mismatched `ScanInputSnapshot.identity` withhold the signal.
- Multiple agreeing or opposing candidates are all shown without ranking or suppression.
- Rejected, unknown, stale, future, wrong-instrument, old-generation, malformed, or digest-mismatched candidates do not appear as signals.
- Typed completed-empty and evaluator-failure cases display different statuses and correctly name the three connected evaluator families.
- Adapter-produced `validUntil` matches each existing quote freshness and event/setup TTL; signal is present at the inclusive deadline and cleared at deadline +1ms, including foreground after a wall-clock jump.
- Signals clear on refresh failure, stale invalidation, instrument/mode change, and backgrounding; relaunch does not restore a signal.
- UI/model tests prove no direction leaks into the Trade decision, plan, CTA, or production notification path; no order call site changes. Include conflicting directional candidates and prove both remain separate, spoofed/missing digests, invalid manifest status, source-input/generation mismatch, expiry, and iPhone/shared behavior unchanged.
- Mac-only UI test/inspection verifies the separate surface and labels. Focused tests, full relevant suite, release `BlazerOSMac` build, `git diff --check`, and independent code review pass.

## Mac evaluator diagnostics — implementation plan and design

The follow-up debate found that the current card loses useful evidence by collapsing a completed empty research scan to one “no candidates” line. The model already retains the evaluator results for the current scan. A Mac-only diagnostic projection can make the empty result useful without manufacturing a directional signal or changing the strategy profile gate.

**Plan:** summarize only the three connected research families (ORB, Candidate001, Candidate002). Represent completion-with-no-emission, rejected/unavailable result, eligible-but-withheld-by-validation, and evaluator failure as distinct states. Never derive a signal from a diagnostic. Keep `Trade WAIT`, selection, plan, notification, CTA, and order flow untouched. iPhone Coach and iPhone UI are explicitly out of scope.

**Design:** diagnostics are ephemeral and tied to the current verified Live scan, instrument, and generation. Clear them wherever the Mac research signal is cleared or its scan becomes invalid. C001/C002 `.completed([])` means “completed · no candidate emitted”; it does not provide a rejection reason. ORB's non-directional evaluation reason may be shown only through an allowlisted reason-to-label mapping. C001/C002 rejection reasons also use an allowlist. An eligible directional candidate that does not pass the signal projector is labeled only “candidate withheld by validation”; its direction, plan, price, and IDs never appear in the diagnostic. Evaluation failures are per-family and display a generic failure label, never raw error text. Legacy policy variants are excluded.

For the empty result state, render three compact, accessible evaluator rows in the Mac card. When candidate cards exist, keep those cards and show the evaluator rows as status context. The rows must remain legible in the fixed Trade layout and say only what the evaluated snapshot supports.
